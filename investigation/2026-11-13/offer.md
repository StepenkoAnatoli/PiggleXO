## Buyer-facing listing draft

**Title:** I will write bilingual customer support replies for your online shop

**Description:**

Do you keep rewriting the same answers about delivery, personalization and order changes?

I will turn your approved store policies and examples of recurring questions into a small set of reusable replies in **Hebrew and either English or Russian**. My background is in customer service and phone sales. This is a new pilot service, and the attached example uses a fictional shop.

For **$149**, the pilot includes:

- 12 reply intents in both selected languages: 24 reply texts in total.
- 5 short FAQ or product-page snippets in both selected languages: 10 texts in total.
- A use guide explaining which facts to check, which placeholders to replace, and when a person must approve the response.
- An editable document and one consolidated revision round within the agreed scope.

Delivery is within **7 calendar days after complete inputs and scope acceptance**. I will ask for your existing policies and up to 20 anonymized examples of common questions. You approve the facts and final wording. Please remove customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, order numbers and payment information before sharing examples.

The package covers written replies for staff to use. Bot installation, live customer support, website changes, legal policies, certified translation and automatic order lookups are outside this package. I cannot promise a particular reduction in messages or increase in sales. Please contact me through the platform before ordering so we can check whether the package fits your shop.

I may use AI tools to help draft and organize the text. I review the final wording against the policies you supply. We agree on any use of your material with an external AI provider before you share it; a manual workflow is possible if you prefer.

**Package settings to review in the actual editor:** one package; 12 reply intents; 5 snippets; 2 languages; 7 calendar days; 1 revision; no express delivery or extra languages; capacity one active order. The platform's terms and mandatory refund rules apply. Do not invent a profile history, qualifications, native fluency, client results, testimonials or availability badge.
