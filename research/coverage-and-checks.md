## Material corrections retained in the final work

1. **Recommendation changed:** generic CSV tool was initially promising; substitute discovery, license ambiguity and unbuilt phone controls demoted it. F3 is the single selected experiment; alternative appendix tests are conditional only.
2. **Template originality removed:** per-event comparison/planning is already sold. The proposed convenience difference is an unproven user-experience hypothesis.
3. **Financial fees corrected:** detailed help takes precedence over a simplified headline; no double counting of subscription full-book acquisition and replacement CAC.
4. **Revenue separated from cash/profit:** processor receivables, reserved refund funds, working cash, owner labor and startup effort are not spendable earnings.
5. **Workload raised where justified:** conversation acquisition adds ~2/9/21 minutes at the F1 base/35-account/optimistic cases. Paused delivery can cost refunds and revenue.
6. **Licenses and APIs narrowed:** public source and working API do not establish commercial permission, account eligibility or sustainable unattended operation.
7. **Source contamination excluded:** builder-led research, vendor fiction, course-pitch replies, shop-wide sales and illustrative spreadsheet numbers were not accepted as verified profit.
8. **Testing claims bounded:** original local tests ran; upstream tests, competitors, actual phones, payments and platform publishing did not. F3 product formulas have not been built/tested yet.
9. **Timing made realistic:** seven days prepares validation; organic acquisition may need 6–8 weeks. A 30-order/eight-week operational pilot can extend beyond the 30-day implementation plan.
10. **Budget stages separated:** initial two-month demand-test allocation is not a limitless operational runway. A later pilot has its own capped decision and must preserve refund liquidity.
