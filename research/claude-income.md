## Bottom line

1. **Sell ChatHai's website chat + Claude-built Hebrew FAQ now, through per-prospect demos, as a paid "founding customer" setup.** The demo pipeline already exists (`ChatHai/outreach`, `node cli.mjs demo`); the web-chat product passes its tests; the WhatsApp/missed-call part waits for Meta and the lawyer. Fastest realistic path to the first ₪.
2. **Fixed-price Hebrew/Russian automation and AI-bot jobs for Israeli small businesses**, using `automation-samples` as the portfolio. Real, posted demand at ₪1,000–10,000 per job, but thin volume and heavy competition.
3. **One or two ad-agency white-label pilots for ChatHai.** Best leverage per conversation, slower to first ₪ because agencies want the WhatsApp piece working.

**Anthropic itself pays nothing to a solo person today:** no affiliate program; the Partner Network targets firms committing to 10 certified people; guest passes have "no cash value" (details in Part 2).

**Legal points that bite here**

- **Doctors' advertising rules shape what the Claude FAQ may say.** The regulations prohibit publishing treatment prices, discounts or promotions, promises that a treatment will succeed, success statistics, and praise of the doctor's skill ([text, Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9D_(%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%AA_%D7%90%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%94)) [weak copy]; listed on [gov.il](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/rofim11) [official]). A doctor also has to stop whoever advertises for them from breaking these rules ([IMA](https://www.ima.org.il/Main/EditClinicalInstruction.aspx?ClinicalInstructionId=256) [official, professional body]). Practical result: the demo prompt in `outreach/src/demo.mjs` and the production FAQ builder should drop prices, discounts, "guaranteed" or "best" wording and outcome claims, and send price questions to a human. Whether a bot answer counts as "advertising" was not verified. Dentists and nurse-run clinics may fall under separate rules (not checked).
- **Section 30A**: one first message to a business, asking for consent, is the only permitted unsolicited electronic ad ([Ministry of Communications FAQ](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/17052018_7) [official]). The outreach tool already enforces this.
- **Charging anyone needs a tax file.** Opening an exempt-dealer file (עוסק פטור) is an online form at no fee ([gov.il](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-open-exempt-dealer-via-internet) [official]); the 2026 turnover ceiling is ₪122,833 ([Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A7_%D7%A4%D7%98%D7%95%D7%A8) [weak]). Check the employment contract for side-work clauses (not researched).
